Biomathematics: Partial Differential Equations - Conducing a numerical analysis

2025
Research project during my curriculum at INSA Lyon, conducted in collaboration with Lucie Galéa and Yasmine Hadra, under the guidance of Dr. Samuel Bernard.
Review of an academic paper theoretical basis and reproduction of the numerical analysis under python. The paper studied (Almeida et al., 2006) was on the application of Finite Element method to a population dynamic problem (Insect migration under different conditions, while accounting for the Allee Effect). The FEM is a common technique to study complex systems in other branches of engineering (e.g.: mechanical, civil, energetical) but is hardly ever used in bioinformatics and biological modelling. I personally worked on the development and implementation of the FEM, which had not been covered in my classes, thus allowing me to expand my numerical modelling and coding skills.

Cellular Biology - Study of the role of H2O2 in murine macrophages

2024
Research project during my curriculum at INSA Lyon, conducted in collaboration with Lucie Galéa, Aurélien Gilquin, and Elsa Vrielynck, under the guidance of Dr. Mélanie Ribeiro-Lopes.
Experimental design and conduction to study the role of H2O2 in the inflammation process. The project was designed to gain greater insight into the cell mechanisms related to inflammation, the research process, and the creation of a research poster. This project was particularly interesting to discover the norms of academic publication and communication, as well as the use of scientific image analysis tools and softwares.
